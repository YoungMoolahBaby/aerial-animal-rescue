# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
The full text at that link applies to everyone who takes part: in issues, pull requests,
discussions, and any other project space.

## In short

- Be welcoming, patient and respectful. People join from many backgrounds and skill levels.
- Critique work, not people. Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- No harassment, discrimination, sexualised language or imagery, trolling, or personal
  attacks.
- Don't publish anyone's private information, including identifiable people or private
  property in footage (see [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md)).
- This project deals with animals in distress. Treat that material, and the people who
  work with it, with care.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour by opening an issue on the
[GitHub repository](https://github.com/YoungMoolahBaby/aerial-animal-rescue/issues) that says
you want to make a private report. **Leave the details out of the issue**: a maintainer will
contact you privately. You can also use GitHub's
[report content](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam)
feature. All reports are reviewed and handled confidentially. Maintainers who do not follow the Code of Conduct
may face consequences decided by the other maintainers.

Enforcement follows the
[Contributor Covenant enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines):
correction, warning, temporary ban, or permanent ban.
